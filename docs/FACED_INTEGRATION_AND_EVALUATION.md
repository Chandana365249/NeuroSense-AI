# FACED integration and evaluation

FACED is a second, separate EEG dataset in NeuroSense AI. This document records how it was validated, how it is
processed, and how the evaluation protocol was fixed before any model was trained. It reports the single held-out result
and lists the limitations and test outcomes.

**Result in one line:** on the strict held-out test (25 unseen participants × 9 unseen film clips, 225 trials), the
selected model reached **56.6% trial-level balanced accuracy** (95% CI 51.8–61.2%). That is better than chance, but
**the 80% target was not reached**.

FACED is kept fully apart from DEAP. The active DEAP model (`valence_binary-random_forest-20261008-180558`), its registry,
the production database and the eight frozen DEAP test participants were not touched, read or used.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Source | NEMAR `nm000112`, version **1.1.4** (BIDS), downloaded by the user as `nm000112_v1.1.4.zip` |
| Licence | CC-BY-4.0 |
| Citation | Chen, J., Wang, X., Huang, C., Hu, X., Shen, X., & Zhang, D. (2023). A Large Finer-grained Affective Computing EEG Dataset. *Scientific Data* 10, 740. doi:10.1038/s41597-023-02650-w |
| Local location | `Dataset/FACED/v1.1.4/` (outside Git; set with `NEUROSENSE_FACED_DATASET_DIR`) |
| Content | 123 healthy adults, 28 emotional film clips each, one continuous 32-channel BDF per participant; `events.tsv` gives each clip's span and the 0–7 self-ratings (including Valence) |

### Validation (`scripts/faced_build.py`, `outputs/faced/validation_report.json`)

- All 750 files in the manifest were extracted and checked against their size and checksum. The ZIP is kept.
- **123 of 123** participants passed validation. Each must have a readable BDF header, events matching the header, a
  known channel layout, a supported sampling rate, microvolt units, 28 clips and ratings within 0–7.
- **3,444 trials**, **24,045** 4-second segments, 63 segments rejected for artifacts (0.26%).
- Amplitude check: the robust standard deviation of 4–45 Hz EEG per participant is 1.84–5.78 µV (median 3.33 µV). Every
  recording is on the same microvolt scale.
- Self-rated valence: 43.3% of trials are above 3.5; 8 trials are exactly 3.5 and count as low.

### Findings that differ from the dataset README

- **Sampling rates.** The README implies 92 participants at 1000 Hz and 31 at 250 Hz. The files actually contain **68 at
  1000 Hz and 55 at 250 Hz**. The pipeline reads each file's own rate.
- **Two channel layouts.** sub-000…060 use the old 10–20 names (T3/T4/T5/T6) and include A1/A2. sub-061…122 use
  T7/T8/P7/P8 and include HEOR/HEOL. Assuming a single layout would have mixed up channels in 62 participants. The
  pipeline maps both layouts by name onto **30 shared scalp channels**. This was found and fixed during validation,
  before any model was trained.

## 2. Signal processing and labels (pipeline `faced-v2`, fixed before training)

For each trial, with no information shared across trials or participants:

1. **Window:** the last 28 s of the clip, read with a 1 s margin on each side. Every clip is at least 34 s long, so each
   trial gives exactly 7 segments of 4 s.
2. **Channels:** the 30 shared scalp channels, in a fixed order.
3. **Common average reference** across the 30 channels.
4. **Resampling:** 1000 Hz recordings are downsampled to 250 Hz with `scipy.signal.resample_poly` (FIR anti-alias
   filter). 250 Hz recordings are used as recorded. The margin is then removed.
5. **Project `EEGPreprocessor`:** 4–45 Hz zero-phase Butterworth band-pass, clipping per channel at ±6 robust SD,
   4 s segments, and rejection of any segment with more than 5% clipped samples.
6. **Project `FeatureExtractor`:** 412 features. Per channel: log and relative band power (theta, alpha, beta, gamma),
   spectral entropy, Hjorth mobility and complexity, and log variance (30 × 12). Plus 13 hemispheric asymmetry pairs ×
   4 bands.
7. **Label:** `high_valence` if self-rated valence is **above 3.5** on the 0–7 scale, otherwise `low_valence`.
8. **Trial prediction:** the mean of the segment probabilities; above **0.5** means high valence.

Learned transforms (scaling and the models) are fitted inside training folds only.

## 3. Evaluation protocol (`faced-protocol-v1`, fixed before training)

Generated by `app/ml/faced/protocol.py` with seed 20261026 and saved as `outputs/faced/protocol_split_v1.json`.
**Split SHA-256:** `01e74a4ed9368203973c252f4ec80ca5fdb2d0d4ed6c6e2a389cab421d163c41`. Every experiment phase checks this
hash and refuses to run if the split has changed.

- **Final-test participants (25):** sub-000, 001, 003, 006, 011, 013, 016, 024, 028, 032, 044, 046, 050, 054, 058, 063,
  073, 077, 096, 105, 107, 110, 111, 117, 121.
- **Final-test clips (9, one per emotion category):** 1, 6, 7, 12, 14, 18, 22, 23, 26.
- **Development:** the other 98 participants × the other 19 clips.
- **Model selection:** 5 inner folds within the development data. Fold *k* validates on participant group *k* × clip
  group *k*. The clip groups are (5, 10, 24, 28), (9, 11, 13, 25), (3, 4, 8, 16), (2, 19, 21, 27) and (15, 17, 20). The
  validation fold's people and clips are never in its training data.
- **Selection metric:** pooled trial balanced accuracy, averaged over the 5 folds. Ties go to the simpler model, in a
  fixed order.

The three settings are reported separately and never combined:

| Setting | Training data | Test data | What it measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1: strict (primary)** | 98 dev participants × 19 dev clips | 25 test participants × 9 test clips | new people **and** new clips |
| S2: unseen people, seen clips | 98 dev participants × all 28 clips | 25 test participants × all 28 clips | new people; clips seen in training (uses the S1-selected model type) |
| S3: strict + unlabelled calibration | as S1, but each person's features are z-scored using that person's own dev-clip segments, with no labels | as S1 | whether per-person normalisation helps |

Candidate models: always-majority, logistic regression (C = 0.01, 0.1, 1), linear SVM (C = 0.1, 1), random forest,
gradient boosting (XGBoost), and a compact EEGNet CNN on raw 4 s segments (S1 only).

### Freeze before the final evaluation

`--phase freeze` wrote `outputs/faced/pre_final_freeze.json` (2026-10-26 16:38 UTC) **before** any test result existed. It
records the selected models, pipeline, label rule, decision threshold and seeds. It also asserts the following, and all
passed:

- inner folds contain no test participant and no test clip;
- S1/S3 training contains no test participant and no test clip;
- S2 training contains no test participant;
- S3 normalisation uses development clips only;
- the strict test set is 25 participants × 9 clips = 225 trials.

`--phase final` refuses to run if `final_results.json` already exists, or if the selection differs from the freeze. It
was **run once**. No model was retuned or reselected afterwards.

### S2: unseen people, seen clips (logistic regression, C = 0.01; 700 trials)

- Balanced accuracy **0.543** (95% CI 0.506–0.576).

- Segment balanced accuracy 0.528.

### S3: strict + unlabelled per-person calibration (random forest; 225 trials)

- Balanced accuracy **0.537** (95% CI 0.478–0.599).
- Accuracy 0.564 (95% CI 0.502–0.627); macro-F1 0.519; ROC AUC 0.567.
- Confusion matrix: [[98, 27], [71, 29]]. It predicts high for only 24.9% of trials.
- Per-participant balanced accuracy: mean 0.519 (95% CI 0.438–0.600).
- Segment balanced accuracy 0.525.

### Was 80% reached?

**No.** Under the strict evaluation, trial-level balanced accuracy is **56.6%**. Even the upper end of its 95% interval
(61.2%) is far below 80%. The other two settings are also far below: 54.3% (S2) and 53.7% (S3).

### Interpretation

- The lower CI bound for pooled S1 balanced accuracy is above 0.5, so there is a weak signal above chance. Accuracy (0.560)
  is almost the same as the always-low baseline (0.556).
- Pooled balanced accuracy (0.566) is higher than the **mean per-participant** balanced accuracy (0.508, CI includes
  0.5). Part of what the model gets right is therefore *between* people: whether a person tends to rate clips high or
  low. Telling one person's high-valence clips from their low-valence clips is hardly better than chance.
- Seeing the clips during training (S2) did not help. Neither did unlabelled per-person calibration (S3), which looked
  best during selection but did not hold up on the test set.
- These results are in line with the low cross-subject accuracies reported elsewhere for binary valence from EEG. They do
  not support describing any individual's emotional state.

## 6. Model artifact and card (separate from DEAP)

- Location: `outputs/faced/models/` (Git-ignored). Files: `faced_S1_lr_C0.01.joblib` (the S1 model used in the app),
  `faced_S2_lr_C0.01.joblib`, `faced_S3_rf.joblib` and `model_card.json`.
- Each artifact is the exact fitted model that produced its reported test scores.
- The card records the following:
  - pipeline `faced-v2` and protocol `faced-protocol-v1` with the split hash;
  - the feature schema: 412 features plus a SHA-256 of the feature names;
  - label rule, preprocessing, training set (98 participants, 19 clips, 1,862 trials), S1/S2/S3 headline results and
    limitations;
  - `target_80_reached: false`;
  - status "EXPERIMENTAL RESEARCH MODEL – not registered, not active, not production-ready; below the 80% target".
- The FACED model is **not** in the DEAP model registry and cannot become the active model. The FACED inference path
  (`FacedService.predict`) refuses to run unless the card's feature count and pipeline version match the FACED pipeline.
  A DEAP-shaped schema is rejected (tested). The DEAP prediction path is unchanged.

## 7. Application integration

- **Backend:** `GET /api/faced/status`, `GET /api/faced/participants?q=`, `GET /api/faced/participants/{id}/trials`,
  `POST /api/faced/predict`. All require sign-in.
  - Only validated participants are listed.
  - Each trial is labelled with how it relates to the evaluation:
    - strict held-out;
    - unseen person, clip seen in training;
    - seen person, unseen clip;
    - in training data.
  - FACED predictions are saved in the user's history as FACED records (since 2026-10-31), with their own provenance.
    They use the existing `predictions` table, so no schema change was needed.
- **Frontend (updated 2026-10-30):** Analyze EEG, Dataset and Model & Evidence share one dataset choice, with the tabs
  "DEAP (active model)" and "FACED (separate research dataset)". The choice is kept in the URL.
  - **Dataset, FACED tab:** validated counts, the `faced-v2` pipeline, the measured held-out performance with "Below the
    80% target", and a searchable directory of participants and recordings. Each recording links into Analyze.
  - **Analyze EEG, FACED tab:** states the dataset, pipeline and model in use; then participant, recording, analysis and
    a result with its evaluation status and "How to read this".
  - **Model & Evidence, FACED tab:** the held-out result, confusion matrix, per-participant results, all settings, model
    selection, exploratory follow-ups and limitations, read from the result files.
  - Changing the participant, clip, dataset or model clears the previous result. Late responses for an earlier selection
    are ignored.

## 8. Reproduction

From `backend/`, with `NEUROSENSE_FACED_DATASET_DIR` pointing at the extracted v1.1.4 folder:

```bash
python -m scripts.faced_build                                 # validate + features (outputs/faced/)
python -m scripts.faced_experiment --phase inner              # classical candidates, 5 inner folds (S1, S3)
python -m scripts.faced_experiment --phase inner_cnn          # EEGNet, 5 inner folds (S1) — ~35 min on CPU
python -m scripts.faced_experiment --phase freeze             # record selection + leakage checks
python -m scripts.faced_experiment --phase final              # held-out test, refuses to run twice
python -m scripts.faced_experiment --phase card               # model card
```

- Seeds: split 20261026; models 42.
- Outputs: `validation_report.json`, `protocol_split_v1.json`, `inner_classical.json`, `inner_cnn.json`,
  `pre_final_freeze.json`, `final_results.json` and `models/`.
- Results were generated from commit `f506e27` plus the uncommitted FACED working-tree changes.

## 9. Limitations

- One protocol, one seed and one held-out run: 25 participants × 9 clips. The confidence intervals are wide.
- Labels are self-reported valence split at the scale midpoint. This is a binary category of what people reported, not
  an emotion detector, and it is **not** diagnostic or clinical.
- Clips were shown to everyone. S1 holds clips out; S2 does not, and is reported only as a separate setting.
- Only the last 28 s of each clip is used; earlier parts and other ratings (arousal, the specific emotions) are not.
- Features are hand-crafted band-power and Hjorth features. The CNN was a single compact configuration with one
  learning-rate and epoch setting, and it was not tuned further.
- The FACED model applies only to FACED recordings processed by this pipeline. It is incompatible with DEAP inputs.

## 11. Model trained on all 123 participants (2026-11-01, exploratory)

The frozen configuration (logistic regression, C = 0.01, `faced-v2` features) was refitted on all 123 participants × 28
clips (3,444 trials) with `python -m scripts.faced_train_all`. There was no tuning. The artifact is in
`outputs/faced/all_participants/`. It is not registered, not active and not used by the application, and the existing
FACED model and results are unchanged.

**This model has no independent test score**, because nobody was held out. The strict held-out 56.6% belongs to the
98-participant model and cannot be claimed for it. Estimates from 5-fold cross-validation over the 123 participants
(seed 20261101) are exploratory, because the earlier test participants had already been examined:

| Estimate | Trials | Trial balanced accuracy (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|
| Unseen people, clips seen in training | 3,444 | 0.534 (0.510–0.556) |
| Unseen people and unseen clips | 690 | 0.493 (0.456–0.532) |
| Training fit, **not** a performance estimate | 3,444 | 0.661 |

The unseen-people-and-clips interval includes chance and lies mostly below the held-out 56.6%. This suggests that the
56.6% may partly reflect which 25 people and 9 clips were held out. It remains the protocol's reported result, and should
be read together with this estimate.
